## Fit and placement development

Base size L, athletic-tall M–4XL is existing brand intent. Actual body measurements, garment points of measure, ease, inseam lengths and grade increments are still required. No manufacturing dimensions or fit approval are implied by the Opus illustration. Offsets and mark widths above are proposals; measure ink bounds, not SVG canvas. Maintain clear space around marks and keep them clear of seams, zipper teeth and high-friction zones. Do not stretch or change the slash geometry.

Use matte stretch transfer as the first application trial. For the jacket, compare an embroidery sew-out only if the chosen fabric supports it without puckering. Do not assume the 20mm mark is embroidery-safe. Test actual substrates for edge fidelity, recovery under stretch, adhesion after washing, comfort and abrasion. Record method, ink/thread, temperature/time/pressure where applicable, sample photos and pass/fail criteria with the vendor before release.
