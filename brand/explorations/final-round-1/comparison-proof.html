F1 — Restrained Connection

Finalist

The timeless build: clean flat-terminal C, a wide engineered M with a controlled center trough, and A2-level restraint in spacing. The S/N link is real but quiet — S's terminal and N's leg-base are joined by a short, low bridge stroke sitting right at the baseline. At a glance it reads as ordinary letter spacing; up close, the bridge is unmistakably a deliberate connector, not a gap.

F2 — Signature Connection

Finalist

The boldest build: clean geometric C, engineered M, and an S whose bottom arm doesn't close into a hook — it flows directly into N's left leg as one continuous stroke (zero gap; horizontally the tail sweeps right into N, vertically it drops straight down into N). Boldest stroke weight (13) and tightest wordmark of the three. This is the strongest proprietary construction, and still reads immediately as CMSN.

F3 — Continuous System

Finalist

Every letter is drawn from one shared rule set: the same ~19-unit corner radius appears in C's aperture and in all four of S's curve corners; the same stroke weight (12) and flat-terminal language run through C, M, S and N; the same round-joint treatment appears at every angle change. The S/N connection uses F1's restrained bridge, drawn with that identical radius — the connector isn't a special case, it's the same rule the rest of the alphabet already follows.
