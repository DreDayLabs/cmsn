A1 — Architectural, baseline

Direction A

The most restrained cut in the family: wide, generous C aperture (radius 20), moderate flat-apex M trough, the established connected-S jog at its original proportions, standard-width N. Reference point the other two A variants refine against.

D1 — Performance, baseline

Direction D

Introduces the family's defining device: a short angled "cut" nub at the C's terminals (forward-motion detail, not decorative), an asymmetric M trough (steeper left leg) and a longer diagonal S jog than any A variant. Bolder stroke (12 vs A's 11). Spacing tightened to 18 units — the widest gap in the D family, since D1 is the introduction of the more aggressive language.

D2 — Performance, S reaches toward N

Direction D

Sharper, tighter C nub; deeper/tighter M trough (18–28 unit flat, deepest of the three); the S's diagonal extends further and its bottom terminal is pulled right toward the N — the closest the terminal comes to "transitioning toward" the next letter while both stay independently legible. Tightest spacing in the family (16 units).

D3 — Performance, steep S with held tail

Direction D

Subtlest C nub of the three (blends toward architectural); narrower, more symmetric M; the S's diagonal is steepest and holds a short vertical beat before curving into the bottom bowl — a different rhythm than D1/D2's continuous sweep. Narrowest N in the family for the tightest overall silhouette.
