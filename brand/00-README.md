# CMSN — Brand Build Playbook

**Brand:** CMSN · **Tagline:** Earn Your CMSN · **Site:** [earnyourcmsn.com](https://earnyourcmsn.com) · **Founder identity:** Dre — The CMSNer

> **Naming rule (locked):** the brand is written **CMSN** — always, everywhere, in every brand context. The spelled-out dictionary word never appears in brand copy, on garments, in captions, or in these docs describing the brand. You *say* the name out loud; you never spell it out. The only exceptions are legal search terms (phonetic-equivalent trademark searches must include the spoken word — see [03](03-legal-and-entity.md)) and literal domain strings.

A fashion brand for the gym. Not performance-first, not luxury-first — *the walk to the gym is the runway.* Built for the covered-up flyness of bigger guys (6'2"+/230+) first, and the "oh, she's going to class" energy next. Black, white, navy, a hint of gray. No pastels. Bronx fresh, corporate comfortable.

4. **Generate the first logo exploration round.** Use the brief and 3 concept directions in [02-identity-and-logo.md](02-identity-and-logo.md) with Adobe Firefly/Illustrator + Claude. *Cost: $0 (you own the tools). Done when: you have 10–15 wordmark candidates on one board.*

## The one-paragraph strategy

CMSN wins by owning a lane nobody in the Alo/Lulu/Gymshark/UA quadrant is serious about: **premium, muted, fashion-grade training wear cut for big men** — and by marketing it through the most honest content format available: a big man who looks incredible walking to the gym, filmed by the guy actually doing it. The name compresses to a four-letter mark that works at every scale; the tagline decodes it and doubles as the community ethic ("you earn everything you get in the gym"). Audience for 90 days, capsule of 4–5 black/white/navy pieces in January when the whole world walks back into the gym, waitlist-driven drop, no discounting. Women's follows once the aesthetic is proven, carried by the credibility of the men's line rather than competing head-on with Lululemon on day one.
