**Margin math sanity check (recommended $10–25k tier):** at these targets, blended landed cost ≈ $13–20/unit against blended retail ≈ $65–75 → **70–75% gross margin**, which is the DTC apparel standard that leaves room for shipping, packaging, payment fees, content costs, and zero-discount discipline. ## The tech-pack process

A tech pack is the contract-in-drawings between you and the factory. One per SKU. Contents:

1. **Cover:** style name/number, season, date, revision number (revision discipline matters — factories build the wrong rev constantly).
2. **Flat sketches** front/back/side with numbered callouts (seams, panels, logo placements, zipper type).
3. **Bill of materials:** fabric (fiber %, GSM, finish), trims (zippers — YKK or equivalent, spec it or get the cheap one), thread, elastic, labels, hangtags.
4. **Points of measure + graded size chart** (the moat — next section).
5. **Construction:** stitch types (flatlock for compression — anything else chafes), seam allowances, hems, bartacks.
6. **Branding placement:** logo method (heat transfer vs. embroidery vs. silicone print), exact position/size per [02-identity-and-logo.md](02-identity-and-logo.md).
7. **Labels & packaging:** care/content/RN label copy ([03](03-legal-and-entity.md)), folding, polybag, mailer.

**Who does what:** Claude drafts the entire pack from your plain-English description (see [06](06-ai-capabilities-map.md)) → a freelance **technical designer reviews and corrects** (~$300–800/style — non-negotiable spend; a bad point-of-measure table costs two sample rounds = 3 months) → factory quotes against the corrected pack. Never quote from a mood image; the pack is what makes quotes comparable.

## The big-and-tall grading moat

Standard menswear grading assumes ~5'10"/180 and scales width faster than length — which is why XXL clothes on a 6'4" athletic body are wide *and short*. The CMSN fit block instead:

- **Base size L, fit-modeled on you** (6'4"/250) — the sample size IS the hero customer, so every sample round is a real fit test.
- Size run **M–4XL** with **athletic drop** (shoulder/chest-to-waist differential kept large through upper sizes instead of barreling), **longer body and sleeve lengths** graded on their own curve, **rise and inseam options** on the compression pant (regular/long inseam at minimum).
- Compression garments need **negative-ease math per size** (typically ~-10 to -15% vs body measurement in stretch directions) — this is exactly the spreadsheet work AI drafts and a technical designer verifies.
- Name the block. "**The 6-4 fit**" or "**CMSN athletic-tall block**" — the fit itself is marketing copy no competitor can paste.

## Fabric guide (what to ask for by name)

| Use | Ask for | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compression pant/top | **Nylon 76–80% / spandex 20–24%**, warp-knit, brushed or matte | 220–300 GSM | Nylon = the premium hand-feel (Lulu/Alo territory); poly/spandex is the cheaper alt — acceptable for v1 if hand-feel passes, but nylon is the "oh, where'd you get that" difference |
| Training short | Poly woven with 4-way mechanical stretch, DWR optional | 120–160 GSM | Lined or unlined — unlined when worn over compression (the CMSN silhouette) |
| Track jacket | Poly/spandex interlock or bonded knit, matte face | 280–350 GSM | Structure is the fashion; flimsy = gym clothes, structured = the Walk. Heavier zipper (YKK #5) |
| Formula tee | Combed cotton or cotton/poly | 180–220 GSM | Heavyweight reads premium; the diagram print needs a smooth face |

**Process discipline:** never approve a fabric from a PDF. Order **swatch cards** first ($0–50), then **lab dips** for the navy (blacks vary too — specify "jet black" and check swatches under daylight), then approve.

## Sourcing path

**Tier 1 (capsule 1): low-MOQ overseas activewear specialists.** China (Guangdong/Fujian activewear clusters, found via Alibaba with filters: verified supplier, activewear-specialized, trade assurance) and Pakistan (Sialkot/Lahore — strong in compression and combat/boxing wear, often the lowest MOQs). Typical startup-realistic terms this tier: **MOQ 50–150 units/style/color** (some accept 30–50 at a per-unit premium), **samples $30–80/piece** (often credited against the order), **production lead 30–60 days** after sample approval, 30–50% deposit / balance before ship.

**Tier 2 (the upgrade path, drop 3+):** Portugal/Turkey — higher MOQs (typically 300+/style) and 2–3× unit cost, but EU-grade quality, better compliance optics, and faster/cheaper shipping to any future EU customers. Move one hero SKU here when volume justifies it; it becomes a "made in Portugal" story piece.

**The vetting funnel (run it exactly like this):**
1. RFQ to **8–10 factories** with the tech pack (Claude drafts the RFQ — [06](06-ai-capabilities-map.md)). Judge reply quality: specific answers, fabric suggestions, pushback = good; instant "yes to everything" = bad.
2. **Swatches + catalogs from the best 4–5.** Kill anyone who can't show existing compression/activewear production.
3. **Paid samples from 2–3** (never just one — you need a comparison and backup). Video-call each factory floor once.
4. **Sample round 2** with corrections on the winner. Expect 2 rounds minimum; budget 3.
5. Production PO with the winner; **third-party inspection** (~$300, QIMA-type) before balance payment on run 1.

**Red flags:** no activewear in their catalog, refusal of video calls, "any MOQ you want," prices 40%+ below the cluster, asking for full payment upfront.
