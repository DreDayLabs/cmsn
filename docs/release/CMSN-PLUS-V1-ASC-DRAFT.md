Description:

CMSN Performance brings training and fueling into one place. Record what you actually did, keep your history, and prepare for your next session.

YOUR TRAINING RECORD
Log exercises, sets, reps and weights. Record a run on iPhone or with the Apple Watch companion. Keep local workout history, your basic CMSN Score and your data export, whether or not you subscribe.

CMSN+ COACHING
Next-set suggestions use your recorded performance, effort and readiness. Each recommendation includes its reason, so you can understand why a target changes.

PLAN AROUND YOUR EQUIPMENT
CMSN+ resolves your program rotation around your equipment profile, with exercise substitutions and quick-session options. This is rule-based session guidance, not an AI coach or a medical assessment.

FUELING FOR PREP AND RECOVERY
Manual food logging and basic macro calculations stay free. CMSN+ adds repeatable prep meal builds, locally filtered high-protein suggestions and training-day fueling adjustments. Nutrition figures are estimates.

YOUR FIRST WEEK
Complete the onboarding profile/assessment to begin seven days of the full system without a payment. CMSN+ continues coaching, planning and fueling after that week. The separate seven-day App Store subscription trial is available only to eligible subscribers.

CMSN+ is $9.99 per month or $59.99 per year in the US, with localized prices shown before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage or cancel through your Apple account's subscription settings. Cancelling does not remove your workout records. Restore Purchases is available in Settings.

Records stay on your device. There is no CMSN account and no cloud sync. Optional calendar, camera, location, motion, Bluetooth and Apple Health permissions support the features you choose to use. CMSN does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
